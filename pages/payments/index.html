❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When will my payment be settled?

Payments are settled in cycles. Each cycle covers 10 days and is settled within 2 days of the cycle end date. You can track your payment status in the transactions section.

How long does bank transfer take?

Once settled, payments are transferred to your registered bank account within 24 hours. NEFT/RTGS ensures secure and fast transfer.

How is the quality bonus calculated?

Quality bonus (0-5%) is based on your harvest quality assessment. Better quality produces higher bonuses up to ₹100 per 100kg.
